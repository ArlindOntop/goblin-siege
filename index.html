Tap any defender card to see its stats, even before you can afford it. Pick one you can afford, then tap an empty grass tile to place it. Tap a placed defender to open its upgrade panel right next to it. Defenders upgrade up to level 5. The purple endgame defenders cost 400+ and are built for the late waves. Bosses: Fisher (wave 5), Bezzgod (10), Crawl (15), Grimtusk (20, raises a shield that blocks all damage), Hexmother (25, splits off goblins as she gets hurt), Gloomwing (30, teleports forward), then stronger repeats on waves 35, 40 and 45. The final boss is MSI Dan on wave 50: he shuts down nearby defenders every 8 seconds and gets angrier at half health. Beat him to win, then keep going in endless mode.
